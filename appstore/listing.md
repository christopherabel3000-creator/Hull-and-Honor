## Description (4,000)

Hull and Honor is a space trading and adventure game about one captain, one ship and a galaxy that remembers everything you do. Haul cargo for the Merchant Guild, run contraband past Concord customs, raid convoys with the pirate clans, or hunt pirates for bounty. Every choice changes who trusts you and who wants you dead.

A LIVING GALAXY
Every campaign builds a new galaxy of about 120 star systems. Famines, plagues, mining booms and blockades swing prices for weeks. Wars break out between the Concord and the pirate clans, front lines shift, and systems change hands. The news keeps you up to date.

YOUR REPUTATION IS EVERYTHING
The Merchant Guild, the Concord and four pirate clans each remember you, and so does every port. Rise high enough and better contracts, ships and story arcs open up. Fall low enough and ports close, bounty hunters come, and a faction that hates you may execute you if you're ever captured.

DEEP, TURN-BASED SHIP COMBAT
Fight at close, medium, long or extreme range. Split your reactor's power between shields, weapons and engines, aim for an enemy's engines or weapons, demand their surrender, board them, or run. Lasers, cannons and missiles each have their best range.

STORIES THAT GROW FROM YOUR CHOICES
Become a navy hero, rise to lead a pirate clan, play both sides as a double agent, win the Merchant Guild's favor, or clear your name when you're wanted everywhere. Stories can collide, and you'll have to pick a side.

A CAPTAIN AND A CREW
Grow nine captain skills, from piloting and gunnery to negotiation and deception. Hire officers with their own personalities, who react to how you run the ship and may ask you for personal favors. Keep your crew paid and loyal, or face desertion and mutiny.

RIVALS, EVENTS AND SURPRISES
Six rival captains travel the galaxy, grow stronger and remember how you treat them. Distress beacons, derelict ships to salvage, ion storms, customs checkpoints and contracts that change partway through mean no two trips are the same.

MORE TO DO
Buy new ships and outfit them with weapons and upgrades. Own warehouses, invest in Guild shipping lines, and build your own station. Play cards, dice or fight in the pits at the cantina.

MADE FOR VOICEOVER
Hull and Honor was built from the start to be fully playable with VoiceOver, by a developer who plays with a screen reader. Every screen is organized with headings, every action is a clearly labeled button, and results are announced once, without repeated instructions.

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
Works offline. No ads. Nothing to buy. No accounts. No tracking. Up to three saved games, or a one life mode for no second chances.

## Notes for the reviewer

Apple asks every new app for six things in this field: a screen recording made on a real iPhone, and answers 2 to 6 below. Only the recording link changes.

Hull and Honor is a complete single-player space trading and adventure game, built to be fully playable with VoiceOver by a developer who plays with a screen reader. The build was tested on a physical iPhone through TestFlight before submission.

1. SCREEN RECORDING
Recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS, starting from launching the app: RECORDING_LINK
The voice heard in the recording is VoiceOver, since the developer plays the game with a screen reader.
The app has no account registration or login, no user-generated content shared with anyone, and no paid content or purchases. So those flows don't exist to show.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
A single-player space trading and adventure game. The player captains one ship in a randomly generated galaxy: trading goods between star systems, taking contracts, fighting turn-based ship battles, and building a reputation with several factions that opens or closes stories, ports and ships. It is for fans of strategy and role-playing games, and especially for blind and low-vision players: it was designed for VoiceOver from the start, with headings on every screen, labeled buttons and short spoken results.

3. HOW TO USE IT
No account, login or sample files are needed. On the title screen, tap Start (names are optional) to begin a campaign. The row of buttons near the top switches between views such as Bridge, Contracts and Market. On the Bridge, tap a jump lane to travel to another system. In the Market, Buy max on a good marked "local product", then sell it where it is "in demand". Fights happen on arrival: tap Fire and maneuver each turn. How to play on the title screen explains everything.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The game is entirely offline: no authentication, payments, ads, analytics, AI services or online play. The only links leaving the app are the optional contact form and privacy pages, which open on the web.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in every region. It is in English only. There is no region-specific content or behavior.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRIES AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry. The optional card and dice games use only in-game credits; there is no real-money gambling and nothing can be bought or cashed out. It contains no third-party or licensed material: the name, artwork, text and code are original.
