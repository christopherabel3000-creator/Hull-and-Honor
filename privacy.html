Privacy policy

Hull and Honor, by playbyear. Last updated November 27, 2026.

Hull and Honor is a single-player space trading and adventure game. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet while you play. This page explains the little information the game handles.

What stays on your device

Your campaigns are saved only on your device: in the browser's storage on the website, and in the app's own storage and Documents folder in the iPhone app. That includes the captain and ship names you type and everything that happens in your game. None of it is ever sent anywhere. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, deletes it.

What the game doesn't do

  • It doesn't collect your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information.
  • It doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • It doesn't send your game anywhere. There are no online features.

Children

The game isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the game ever handles information differently, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions, problems, or anything about your privacy: use the contact form, and say that it's about Hull and Honor. You don't need an account, and only the game's developer sees what you send. Add your email address only if you'd like a reply; it's used just to answer you. Messages are kept in Google Forms, under Google's terms.
