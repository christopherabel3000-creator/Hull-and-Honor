Help and support

Hull and Honor, by playbyear.

Getting in touch

Use the contact form for bug reports, questions and ideas, and say that it's about Hull and Honor. You don't need an account. Add your email address if you'd like a reply. For a bug, it helps to say what you were doing and whether you use VoiceOver.

Playing with VoiceOver

Hull and Honor was built to be played with a screen reader. Each port screen has a row of view buttons near the top: Bridge, Contracts, Market, Shipyard, Captain, Crew, Holdings, Cantina, Rivals, News and Records. Each view starts with a heading, so the headings rotor moves quickly between sections. Results of what you do, like a trade or a combat turn, are announced once.

Learning the game

Choose How to play on the title screen, or in the Records view, for a short guide to travel, trading, reputation, combat, your captain and crew, and what happens when you lose.

Your saved games

The game saves automatically after everything you do. You can also keep up to three saved games in the Records view, unless you chose one life mode. In the iPhone app, your saves are also kept in the app's Documents folder, so they survive even if iOS clears the app's web storage, and they're included in your phone's backups.

Saves stay on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and nothing leaves your device. See the privacy policy.
